01 / THE RESEARCH

Abstract.

Vocal-to-accompaniment generation (V2A) aims to generate instrumental accompaniment that musically matches a given vocal performance. Existing V2A methods typically take as input either vocal audio representations or simplified melody representations extracted from the same vocal. The former entangle melody with timbre and other acoustic factors, while the latter discard potentially useful vocal context.

Moreover, explicit optimization for vocal–accompaniment compatibility remains limited. In this study, we propose MEGA, a MElody-Guided and Harmony-Aligned V2A framework built upon the pretrained ACE-Step 1.5. MEGA injects melody cues into the backbone’s vocal-conditioned hidden states through a zero-gated cross-attention plugin, preserving rich vocal context while providing explicit melodic guidance.

Factorized guidance independently adjusts text and melody contributions to balance text-driven arrangement with melodic adherence. We further employ harmony-aware direct preference optimization to improve both musical quality and vocal–accompaniment compatibility. Experimental results show that MEGA outperforms the compared V2A systems in overall musical quality and vocal–accompaniment compatibility. Audio demos are available at https://aslp-lab.github.io/MEGA/, and the code will be released as open source.
